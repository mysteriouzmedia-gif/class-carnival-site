About this tool

A Christmas race timer for the classroom: Santa with his sack of presents, a busy elf, a reindeer, a snowman, a gingerbread cookie, a candy cane and a penguin in a Santa hat race through a snowy village at night, and the order they finish becomes your turn order. It’s festive and friendly, so it works from kindergarten right up through middle school.

Use it through December for presentation order, reading turns, gift-exchange order, class party games, or deciding who answers first in a Christmas vocabulary review. ESL classes can use the runners as vocabulary too — Santa, elf, reindeer, snowman, gingerbread, candy cane, penguin.

Paste in your class list or load a saved class, press start, and switch to fullscreen for the projector. Every lane fits on screen however big the class, there’s no sign-up, and every race is different.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Christmas Race Timer free?

Yes. The Christmas Race Timer is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

How does the race decide the order?

Every race is random. The runners speed up and slow down unpredictably, so nobody knows who will win until the end. The finishing order becomes your turn order — for presentations, reading turns, lining up, or who answers first.

How many students can race?

You can race the whole class. The lanes resize to fit however many names you enter, and each runner’s name tag stays readable on a projector.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
