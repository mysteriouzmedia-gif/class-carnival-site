About this tool

Deciding who goes first can eat up more class time than it should — and hand-picking always risks looking unfair, especially in a classroom where students notice patterns fast. This race timer solves that with a fully randomized visual race: type in your class list, hit start, and watch it play out in real time instead of just generating a flat, boring list.

It works well for presentation order, reading turns, who answers first in a Q&A drill, or line-up order at the end of class. Because the whole class watches the race happen, the result feels transparent and fair — nobody can accuse you of favoritism when the order was decided by an animated race they watched unfold themselves.

Paste in as few as two names or as many as your whole roster; there's no artificial cap. Runs entirely in your browser, no account needed, and results are different every time you race — reset instantly for a fresh order whenever you need one.
