About this tool

A themed classroom stopwatch that's big enough to read from the back of the room — and fun enough that students want to watch it. Choose a theme: a runner who laps the track once every minute, a garden that sprouts a new flower every 30 seconds, a rocket that flies past the Moon, Mars and beyond as time goes on, or a classic big-digit stopwatch.

Turn on challenge mode to make clean-up time, lining up, or getting ready into a game: the class tries to beat its fastest time, and a new record gets a celebration. The record is remembered in your browser, so you can bring the challenge back tomorrow. Use the lap button to record several times in a row — handy for reading fluency checks, relay drills, or timing students one after another.

A timer buddy cheers the class on and calls out each minute with a bell, and every theme has its own sounds — footsteps and a cheering crowd on the track, birdsong in the garden, zaps and whooshes in space. Switch theme sounds off any time. In challenge mode a progress bar shows how close the class is to its record time, so everyone can see if they're on pace.

Press Space to start and stop, L to record a lap, and R to reset. Works in fullscreen on any projector, with no sign-up or download.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Classroom Stopwatch free?

Yes. The Classroom Stopwatch is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

What is challenge mode?

Challenge mode turns clean-up, lining up or any routine into a game: the class tries to beat its fastest time. A progress bar shows how close they are, and a new record gets a celebration. The record is remembered in your browser.

Can I record laps?

Yes. Press Lap (or L) to record split times — handy for timing reading fluency, relay drills, or several students one after another.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. The speaker button in the top-left corner mutes every sound on the site, and your choice is remembered the next time you visit.
