About this tool

Captain’s Draft picks teams the way students do on the playground — but fairly. First, choose the captains: click a name, or let the random picker flicker across the class and land on someone. Then the draft begins: each captain takes a turn choosing a teammate by clicking a name card, and the card flies into their team.

Snake order (on by default) means the captain who picked last in one round picks first in the next, so no team gets all the early picks. Shy captain, or want to avoid hurt feelings? Press “Surprise Pick” and the tool picks a random player for them — or “Auto-Draft the Rest” to finish instantly. Teams always come out within one player of each other.

Great for PE, sports-style games, debates, and team competitions. Copy the finished teams with the captains marked, and save your class list so it’s ready on every ClassCarnival tool. Free and no sign-up.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Captain’s Draft free?

Yes. The Captain’s Draft is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Is a captain’s draft fair to the students picked last?

The tool helps: snake order means the captain who picked last in one round picks first in the next, and the “Surprise Pick” button lets any captain pick at random. Teams always end up within one player of each other in size.

Can I copy the teams afterwards?

Yes. Press “Copy Teams” to copy every team and its members as text, ready to paste into a document, chat or slide.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
