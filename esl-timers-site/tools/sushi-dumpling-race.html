About this tool

A food-themed spin on the random order race, alternating sushi and dumpling racers as they make their way to the finish line. Same fair, fully random mechanic as the classic Race Timer — type in your student list, hit start, and let the race decide who goes first.

Good for adding a bit of visual variety when you've already used the classic race or another themed one recently, or simply for classes that respond well to food-themed visuals. Works for the same practical uses as every race on this site: deciding presentation order, reading turns, who answers first, or line-up order at the end of class.

No sign-up, no limit on class size, and a different random order every time you race — reset and run it again as many times as your lesson needs.
