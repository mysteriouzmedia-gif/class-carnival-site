About this tool

A food-themed twist on the random order race — same fair, fully randomized mechanic as the classic Race Timer, with a cast of distinct, smiling characters (nigiri, a maki roll, an onigiri rice ball, a gyoza dumpling, and a soup dumpling) racing through a lantern-lit night market toward the finish line instead of plain icons. Paste in your student list, hit start, and let the tasty crew decide who goes first.

Works for anything you'd use the classic Race Timer for: presentation order, reading turns, deciding who answers first, or picking a line-up order at dismissal. The food theme tends to land well with classes that respond to something a bit different from the usual animal or fantasy themes.

As with every race on this site, there's no limit on class size, no account required, and results are different every single time — reset and race again as many times as you like during a lesson.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Sushi & Dumpling Race Timer free?

Yes. The Sushi & Dumpling Race Timer is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

How does the race decide the order?

Every race is random. The runners speed up and slow down unpredictably, so nobody knows who will win until the end. The finishing order becomes your turn order — for presentations, reading turns, lining up, or who answers first.

How many students can race?

You can race the whole class. The lanes resize to fit however many names you enter, and each runner’s name tag stays readable on a projector.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
