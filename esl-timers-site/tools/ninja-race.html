About this tool

A ninja-themed spin on the random order race — same fair, fully randomized mechanic, dressed up for classes that respond well to a bit of stealth-and-speed theming. Paste in your student list, hit start, and watch a field of ninjas race toward the finish line to decide who goes first.

Works for anything you'd use the classic Race Timer for: presentation order, reading turns, deciding who answers first, or picking a line-up order at dismissal. The ninja theme tends to land especially well with classes in the middle of an action, adventure, or hero-adjacent unit, or simply with students who respond to a bit of extra visual flair over a plain list.

As with every race on this site, there's no limit on class size, no account required, and results are different every single time — reset and race again as many times as you like during a lesson.
