About this tool

The Seating Chart Shuffle builds a random seating plan for your classroom in seconds. Set how many desks go across and how many rows you have, and the name cards are dealt out from the teacher’s desk to each seat — a fun reveal on the projector when students come in on seat-change day.

Add “keep apart” pairs and the shuffle makes sure those students never sit next to each other (left, right, in front, or behind). Choose desks in pairs, fill the front rows first, then fine-tune by clicking any two desks to swap them. Your layout is remembered for next time.

When you’re happy, print the chart for your desk or substitute folder, or copy it as text. Save your class list once and it’s ready on every ClassCarnival tool. Free, no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded — everything stays in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Seating Chart Shuffle free?

Yes. The Seating Chart Shuffle is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Can I keep certain students apart?

Yes. Open “Keep students apart” and list pairs, one per line. The shuffle makes sure they don’t sit next to each other — left, right, in front or behind — and warns you if the layout makes that impossible.

Can I print the seating chart?

Yes. Press Print for a clean, ink-friendly version of the chart, or Copy Chart to paste it as text. You can also click any two desks to swap students before printing.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
