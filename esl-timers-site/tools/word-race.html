About this tool

Most random pickers only work with names — this one is built to race anything you type in, one item per line, which makes it useful well beyond turn-order. Paste in a vocabulary list instead of student names, and the race decides which word your class reviews first, second, third, and so on, turning a plain vocabulary drill into something with a bit of visible suspense.

It's a natural fit for warm-up reviews, spelling practice, or randomizing the order of a "word of the day" rotation across the week. Because it's the same underlying engine as the classic Race Timer, it works exactly as well with student names if you'd rather use it that way — there's nothing vocabulary-specific under the hood, just a different way of framing what you type in.

As always: any list length works, no sign-up required, and every race produces a different order, so review sessions don't get predictable.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Word Race Timer free?

Yes. The Word Race Timer is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Can I race vocabulary words instead of names?

Yes — that is what the Word Race is for. Enter your vocabulary list and the race decides which word the class reviews first, second and third. It works with student names too.

How can I use it in an ESL lesson?

Use the finishing order as the order to review, spell, act out or make sentences with each word. Students love guessing which word will win before the race starts.

Will it work on my classroom projector or whiteboard?

Yes. Press the fullscreen button in the top-right corner and the tool fills the screen, with text big enough to read from the back of the room. It also works on interactive whiteboards and tablets.
