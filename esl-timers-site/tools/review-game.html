About this tool

A free classroom review game that does the setup for you. Paste in your class list, choose how many teams you want, and ClassCarnival deals everyone into balanced teams with names and mascots. Every turn, the game picks the team and a student from that team to answer — fairly, so nobody is picked twice until everyone on their team has had a go.

Two ways to play

Team Race: each correct answer moves your team's mascot one step along the track, and a streak of three correct answers in a row gives a double step. The first team across the finish line wins. Bomb Board: the classic ESL bomb game — teams take turns choosing a numbered tile, which might hide 10, 20 or 30 points, a bomb, a swap, a steal, a double or a freeze. The highest score when the board is cleared wins.

With or without questions

Paste in your own questions (with optional hidden answers) and they'll appear on screen one at a time, or skip that step and ask your questions out loud — then just tap Correct or Incorrect. It works for vocabulary review, grammar practice, maths facts, test prep, or any quiz, with no sign-up and no student devices needed.
