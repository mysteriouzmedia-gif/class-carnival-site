About this tool

A gacha machine name picker for the classroom — the capsule toy machines kids know from shops and arcades. A coin drops in, the dial clicks round, and a two-colour capsule tumbles into the tray. Then it pops open to reveal whose turn it is. It uses the same fair no-repeat system as every picker on this site: once a student's capsule has come out, they're set aside until everyone else has had a turn.

Turn on surprise prizes and each capsule also hides a little extra — most are common, but some are ✨ rare and a lucky few are 👑 legendary, complete with a special glow and fanfare. Use the prize as a fun bonus (the legendary student picks the next song, sits in the special chair, or gets a sticker), or just enjoy the suspense. Every capsule lands in the class collection so you can see who's been picked.

Great for cold-calling, choosing who answers next, turn order, or classroom jobs — and especially popular with younger learners and ESL classes. Paste in your class list or load a saved class, and use fullscreen mode on the projector. No sign-up needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Gacha Machine Name Picker free?

Yes. The Gacha Machine Name Picker is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Will the same student be picked twice?

No. Every student is picked once before anyone is picked again. When the whole class has had a turn, a fresh round starts automatically, so cold-calling stays fair.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.

Will it work on my classroom projector or whiteboard?

Yes. Press the fullscreen button in the top-right corner and the tool fills the screen, with text big enough to read from the back of the room. It also works on interactive whiteboards and tablets.
