About this tool

A themed classroom countdown timer that students actually watch. Choose a theme to match the moment: a rocket whose fuel drains until it blasts off, a balloon that swells until it pops, an ice cream that slowly melts, a classic sand timer, or a simple big-digit timer with a colour-changing progress bar. Every theme is readable from the back of the room and works in fullscreen on a projector.

In the final 10 seconds, giant numbers count down on screen (with an optional tick sound), so students get a clear cue to wrap up even when the room is noisy. When time's up, each theme has its own finale — liftoff, a pop, a puddle — followed by a big "Time's up!" message.

To keep the class engaged, a timer buddy cheers everyone on, calls out halfway and the last minute, and starts to panic when time is nearly gone. Each theme has its own sound effects — the rocket rumbles, the ice cream drips, the balloon gets pumped — with alerts at halfway, one minute and thirty seconds left, and a heartbeat in the final countdown. Turn theme sounds off any time and keep just the alerts. Turn on beat-the-timer mode and press “We’re done!” when the class finishes early to unlock a mystery reward you choose.

Use it for independent work, timed writing, group discussions, test sections, clean-up time, or transitions. Pick a preset (30 seconds to 15 minutes) or set any custom time, add an extra minute on the fly, and press Space to start and pause. No sign-up and no download needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Classroom Countdown Timer free?

Yes. The Classroom Countdown Timer is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

What themes does the countdown timer have?

Rocket Launch, Balloon Pop, Melting Ice Cream, Sand Timer, Classic, and two Christmas themes — a Melting Snowman and a Present to unwrap. Each has its own animation, sounds and finale, plus a big 10-second countdown at the end.

What is beat-the-timer mode?

Turn it on and a “We’re done!” button appears. If the class finishes before time runs out, they win a random mystery reward from a list you can edit.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. The speaker button in the top-left corner mutes every sound on the site, and your choice is remembered the next time you visit.
