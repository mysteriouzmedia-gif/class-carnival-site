About this tool

The Classroom Screen puts the tools you use every lesson onto one projector screen: a big clock, a countdown timer, a random name picker, a live noise meter, work-mode signs, a traffic light and a space for today’s instructions. Switch widgets on and off to build the screen you need — just a timer and instructions for a test, or the noise meter and work mode for group work — then press fullscreen.

The work-mode sign shows students exactly what’s expected right now: silent work, whisper voices, partner talk, group work, “ask three before me”, or eyes on the teacher. The traffic light gives a quick visual signal for stop, get ready and go, and the name picker never repeats a student until everyone has had a turn.

Your layout, background, instructions and class list are remembered in your browser, so the screen is ready when you come back. The noise meter measures sound live on your computer — nothing is recorded or uploaded. Free, no sign-up, and it works on any laptop connected to a projector or interactive whiteboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Classroom Screen free?

Yes. The Classroom Screen is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

What can I put on the classroom screen?

A clock, a countdown timer, a random name picker, a noise meter, work-mode signs, a traffic light and a box for instructions. Turn each widget on or off and choose a background colour.

Will my screen be saved for next time?

Yes. Your widgets, background, instructions and class list are remembered in your browser, so the screen is ready when you open it again.

Will it work on my classroom projector or whiteboard?

Yes. Press the fullscreen button in the top-right corner and the tool fills the screen, with text big enough to read from the back of the room. It also works on interactive whiteboards and tablets.
