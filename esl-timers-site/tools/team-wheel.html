About this tool

A team spinner wheel for the classroom. Choose how many teams you want and the wheel fills with team colours and mascots. Then, one at a time, each student's name comes up and the wheel spins to decide their team — the kind of moment that gets the whole room cheering (or groaning).

Unlike a plain spinner, the Team Wheel keeps things fair: the teams are balanced behind the scenes, so they always end up within one student of each other in size, and a team that's full is marked on screen. Short on time? "Spin Everyone" whizzes through the rest of the class in seconds.

Use it to split the class for games, projects, or competitions, then take your teams straight into the Review Game. Copy the finished teams with one click, and save your class so it's ready on every ClassCarnival tool. No sign-up needed.
