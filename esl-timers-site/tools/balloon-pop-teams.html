About this tool

Balloon Pop Teams is a random team maker for younger learners. The sky fills with one balloon per student, each hiding a name. A student taps any balloon, it pops, and the name inside drops straight into its team’s basket — so every pop is a double surprise: who was inside, and which team they’re on.

Teams are balanced automatically, so they always end up within one student of each other in size. Choose 2 to 8 teams, each with its own colour and mascot. Pass the mouse around and let students pop balloons themselves, press Space to pop one at random, or use “Pop Them All” when you’re in a hurry.

Perfect for kindergarten and elementary classes, ESL lessons, PE, and party days. Copy the finished teams in one click and save your class list so it’s ready on every ClassCarnival tool. Free, no sign-up, and made for the projector or interactive whiteboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Balloon Pop Teams free?

Yes. The Balloon Pop Teams is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Are the teams balanced?

Yes. Teams always end up within one student of each other in size, and every team gets its own colour and mascot.

Can I copy the teams afterwards?

Yes. Press “Copy Teams” to copy every team and its members as text, ready to paste into a document, chat or slide.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
