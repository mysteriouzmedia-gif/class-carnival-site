About this tool

The Magic Team Sorter turns making teams into a show. One by one, each student is called up and the Magic Hat thinks it over out loud — “Hmm, a tricky one… I sense great ideas!” — with a drumroll, before shouting out their team: “TEAM DRAGONS!” Every so often it changes its mind at the last second, which students love.

Behind the scenes the teams are always balanced, so they end up within one student of each other in size no matter what the hat says. Choose 2 to 8 teams, each with its own colour and mascot, and use “Sort Everyone” when you’re short on time.

Great for the start of a unit, sports day, class competitions, or splitting the room for a review game. Copy the finished teams with one click, and save your class list so it’s ready on every ClassCarnival tool. Free, no sign-up, and made for the projector.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Magic Team Sorter free?

Yes. The Magic Team Sorter is completely free — no sign-up, no student accounts and nothing to download. It runs in any modern web browser on a laptop, tablet, interactive whiteboard or projector.

Does the hat really decide the teams?

The hat’s thinking and drumroll are for fun — behind the scenes the teams are planned to be balanced, so they always end up within one student of each other in size.

Can I copy the teams afterwards?

Yes. Press “Copy Teams” to copy every team and its members as text, ready to paste into a document, chat or slide.

Can I save my class list?

Yes. Type or paste your names once, then save them as a class in the “My classes” bar above the names box. Your saved classes load on every ClassCarnival tool. They are stored only in your own browser — nothing is uploaded.
