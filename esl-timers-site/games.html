Team games for the whole class

These tools are built for one shared screen at the front of the room. The Group Maker deals your class into balanced random groups or teams — with team names and mascots, and a one-at-a-time reveal for extra suspense. The Review Game goes a step further: it makes the teams for you, picks which student answers each turn, and keeps score.

Which one should I use?

Use the Group Maker when you just need groups for pair work, projects, or stations. Use the Review Game for vocabulary review, grammar practice, or test prep — play it as a Team Race, where correct answers move each team's mascot to the finish line, or as the classic ESL Bomb Board with points, bombs, swaps, steals, doubles and freezes. Team Race is a simple click-to-advance race when you're keeping score your own way.

Fair for every student

Teams are always balanced to within one student, and in the Review Game every student on a team gets a turn to answer before anyone repeats. Save your class once and it's ready on every ClassCarnival tool.
