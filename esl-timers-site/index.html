Why teachers use ClassCarnival

Every tool here is built for a shared classroom screen: big text, fullscreen mode, and nothing to install or sign up for. Paste in your class list once and you can run a race, a name picker, or a timer in seconds.

The name pickers and races are designed around fairness. Instead of pulling a random name every time — which means some students get picked again and again — each tool works through the whole class before anyone repeats. That makes cold-calling feel like a game rather than a gotcha, which matters most for younger learners and students speaking in a second language.

Pick the style that suits your class: a quick list picker when you just need a name, a gumball machine or popsicle stick cup when you want a bit of suspense, or a themed race when you need to decide presentation order and want the whole room watching.
