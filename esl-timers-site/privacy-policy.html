Privacy Policy

Last updated: [08/08/2026]

What this site does with your data

ClassCarnival's tools (race timers, name pickers, stopwatch, countdown) run entirely in your browser. Any names, words, or lists you type into a tool stay on your device — they are not sent to, stored on, or processed by our servers. Closing or refreshing the page clears everything you typed.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand which pages and tools are used, so we know what to build or improve next. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect information such as your approximate location (derived from IP address), browser type, device type, and pages visited. This data is aggregated and not used to personally identify you.

This site also uses (or plans to use) Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, at Google's Advertising Privacy & Terms page.

No accounts, no sign-up

ClassCarnival does not require or offer account creation, login, or sign-up. We do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information directly from visitors through any form on this site.

Children's privacy

ClassCarnival is a tool intended for use by teachers in a classroom setting — it is not directed at children, and children are not expected to interact with the site or input their own data directly. Any student names a teacher types into a tool are entered by the teacher, stay in-browser only as described above, and are never transmitted or stored by this site. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings, and you can control or disable cookies through your browser's settings at any time. Disabling cookies may affect how analytics and ads function but will not affect your ability to use the classroom tools themselves.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site grows. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "last updated" date.
